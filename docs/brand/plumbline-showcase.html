Four principles

Every decision in this system traces back to one of these. When a token does not cover a case, the principles decide.

01

The measurement is the hero

The plumb line, landmark dots, and angle arcs are designed brand assets, not debug output. They carry the visual identity the way a point cloud carries Neko Health's.

02

Engineered calm

Premium is whitespace, editorial type, and restraint. Nothing pure black, nothing pure white, nothing neon. Energy comes from precision, never from saturation.

03

Paper first

The report a practitioner hands a client is the most important brand surface. If it would look wrong printed on porcelain paper, it is wrong.

04

Color means something

One interactive accent. Risk colors appear only when they encode risk. Copper appears only in marketing. Decoration never borrows a semantic color.

The plumb line

Posture assessment has used a weighted vertical string as its reference for over a century. We digitized the instrument; the brand keeps the line. A 1.5px hairline, an ink-blue bob, copper landmarks, and Plex Mono degree readouts form the entire graphic language.

Color

Two rooms, one house. Porcelain is the default world: reports, marketing, light app. Ink is the dark app theme and the dark advertising canvas. The territories we deliberately avoid: healthcare green, trust teal, purple, black plus neon, and the old indigo.

Ink, the dark room

Dark mode lightens every semantic color to hold contrast. One hard rule: in dark mode, risk chips are tinted surfaces with the risk color as text. White text on dark-mode risk fills fails contrast and is banned.

Motion

Instrument motion, not entertainment. Lines draw, arcs sweep, values count up once. Everything else holds still.

Easing

cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

One curve everywhere. Spring physics only for tactile press feedback.

Duration

150ms press · 250ms hover · 450ms reveal · 1600ms line draw

Nothing loops forever. A drawn plumb line draws once.

Reduced motion

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)

All draws and reveals collapse to instant static states. Non-negotiable.

Do's and don'ts

  • Make the measurement overlay the hero of every marketing surface.
  • Set every number that can change in IBM Plex Mono, slashed zero on.
  • Keep one interactive accent per screen; risk colors only encode risk.
  • Use screening vocabulary: maintain, warning, danger; optimal to significant.
  • Design the report and the ad in the same sprint, from the same tokens.
  • No pure black, no pure white, no healthcare green, no purple, no old indigo.
  • No copper inside assessment or report surfaces.
  • No grades or risk states encoded by color alone.
  • No Schibsted Grotesk below 21px, no third weight of any family.
  • No card shadows; hairlines and tonal steps carry all depth.
