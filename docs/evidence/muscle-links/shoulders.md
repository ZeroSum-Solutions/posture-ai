# Literature Verdicts — Shoulder Imbalances (lit-p2)

## anterior_imbalanced_shoulders (rounded/protracted/anteriorly tilted)

- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / anterior-deltoid [tight]: confidence=low | citation="Janda 1988 / Kendall 2005 — textbook inference (upper-crossed); no EMG study isolating anterior-deltoid overactivity in rounded-shoulder posture found."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / lower-trapezius [weak]: confidence=high | citation="Gu et al. 2024 (J Orthop Surg Res, PMC11214228) — EMG confirmed reduced lower-trapezius activity in adults with rounded-shoulder posture; consistent with Cools et al. 2007 scapular-dyskinesis findings."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / middle-trapezius [weak]: confidence=medium | citation="Bae et al. 2023 (Int J Environ Res Public Health, PMC9956189) — RCT; trapezius strengthening corrected rounded-shoulder posture; middle-trap weakness consistently cited alongside lower-trap but no standalone EMG study found."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / pectoralis-major [tight]: confidence=medium | citation="Lewis et al. 2010 (Man Ther, PMID 20850039) — manual therapy targeting anterior chest reduced rounded-shoulder angle; pec-major shortening mechanism cited; no isolated pec-major EMG overactivity study found."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / pectoralis-minor [tight]: confidence=high | citation="Borstad & Ludewig 2005 (JOSPT, PMID 15901124) — shorter pec-minor resting length correlated with greater anterior scapular tilt and internal rotation during arm elevation in healthy adults."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / rhomboids [weak]: confidence=low | citation="Janda 1988 / upper-crossed model — textbook inference; rhomboids listed as weak retractors; no EMG study isolating rhomboid underactivity in adults with rounded-shoulder posture found."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / serratus-anterior [weak]: confidence=high | citation="Pirauá et al. 2014 (PMID 24997890) — adults with scapular dyskinesis showed decreased serratus-anterior EMG; supported by PMC11145323 motor-unit study showing altered SA recruitment in scapular dyskinesis."
- anterior_imbalanced_shoulders / upper-trapezius [tight]: confidence=high | citation="Mahmoud et al. 2023 (PMC10454745, systematic review) — upper-trapezius overactivity consistently found in upper-crossed/scapular-dyskinesis posture; decreased after corrective exercise."

## posterior_imbalanced_shoulders (scapulae retracted/depressed)

- posterior_imbalanced_shoulders / levator-scapulae [tight]: confidence=medium | citation="Mahmoud et al. 2023 (PMC10454745, systematic review) — levator scapulae listed as overactive in upper-crossed pattern; direct EMG evidence specific to posterior/retracted scapular posture not found."
- posterior_imbalanced_shoulders / lower-trapezius [weak]: confidence=high | citation="Gu et al. 2024 (J Orthop Surg Res, PMC11214228) — lower-trapezius underactivity is a consistent EMG finding in scapular dyskinesis; same evidence base applies to posterior presentation."
- posterior_imbalanced_shoulders / upper-trapezius [tight]: confidence=high | citation="Mahmoud et al. 2023 (PMC10454745, systematic review) — upper-trapezius overactivity consistent across shoulder-girdle imbalance presentations; same evidence applies to posterior context."

## Notes

- **anterior-deltoid [tight]**: The upper-crossed literature names anterior deltoid as part of the tight-anterior chain but every dedicated EMG study targets pec minor and pec major. No study was found measuring anterior deltoid overactivity or shortening as a primary driver in rounded-shoulder adults. Confidence remains low.
- **rhomboids [weak]**: Consistently named in narrative/systematic reviews as underactive retractors in rounded-shoulder posture, but landmark EMG studies (Cools, Pirauá, Gu) quantify lower trap and serratus anterior — not rhomboids independently. Grade is low pending a dedicated rhomboid-EMG study in this posture.
- **middle-trapezius [weak]**: Evidence exists but is indirect (grouped with lower trap in RCTs and reviews). A standalone EMG paper comparing middle-trap activation between rounded-shoulder and control adults would elevate this to high.
- **posterior_imbalanced_shoulders (all three links)**: This specific posture pattern (retracted/depressed scapula) is less studied as a distinct syndrome than rounded-shoulder. Evidence is extrapolated from upper-crossed syndrome and general scapular dyskinesis literature; all three grades carry this caveat.
- **Tally**: high=6 | medium=3 | low=2
