## Inspection boundary

The live wger API supplied exact exercise, equipment, author-history, license and media records. Direct browser pixel inspection of its three candidate images could not be completed in this pass: the available Mac browser was locked, and those asset URLs were not present in indexed image search. No visual claim about those pixels is inferred from the exercise description. The direct links above are intentionally preserved so a reviewer can finish the side-by-side check without rediscovering the records.

The indexed comparison images were used only to make the variant checklist concrete. Their pages do not provide a verified reusable-media license, so they must not be imported or substituted for the wger assets.

## Catalog decision

Do not wire any of these records into the production catalog yet. Exercise 203 is the strongest rights-and-variant candidate but still needs a direct two-frame visual check. Exercise 1652 needs confirmed setup/movement coverage. Walking 1104 has no media; the Commons loop is a rights-documented fallback whose source resolution is below the desired quality bar.

Load semantics remain fixed: goblet squat is one physical dumbbell held by two hands and must never become a per-hand or doubled-tonnage label. Romanian deadlift is one dumbbell per hand, two implements total. Media approval cannot change those programming contracts.
