# Posture AI design renewal loop

## Design renewal loop

This project loop refreshes one user-facing surface at a time, preserving
clinical behavior while progressively replacing the retired visual system;
it stops once the new design contract, product shell, marketing surface, and
priority authenticated flows pass visual, accessibility, and regression checks.

Prompt:
> Observe the current surface and its acceptance checks, choose the highest-impact in-scope visual improvement, implement one reversible slice using the Posture AI Dark tokens, then verify at desktop and mobile sizes plus the relevant automated checks. Record evidence and continue only while each pass improves the product. Stop when the defined surface passes, no measurable improvement remains, or approval is needed for scope, data, or deployment changes.
