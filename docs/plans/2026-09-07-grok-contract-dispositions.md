# Grok review dispositions and contract decisions

Status: lead decisions for implementation; access/eligibility schema is still being reconciled with the data owner. These clarify the approved PRD. They do not establish clinical validity or activate unreviewed production content. External review: `2026-09-07-strength-conditioning-grok-review.md`.

## Accepted programming clarifications

- **Onboarding history (WARN-2):** recalled/imported working sets may inform a familiarization suggestion but never enter the two-exposure progression window. Only completed, acknowledged in-app exposures with immutable source revisions qualify. Calibration and actual performance remain distinct.
- **Comparator (WARN-3):** a load increase requires both most recent qualifying exposures in the current series/load epoch, each with every prescribed working set at one identical load, ceiling reps and acceptable effort. Mixed working loads produce `mixed_working_load_review`; they do not silently discard the difficult sets. Rep progression uses only the most recent qualifying exposure and assigns one total rep to its earliest below-ceiling set. One exposure at a new load prevents a load increase, but does not independently forbid the valid one-rep branch. No progression from a still-running/aborted session or an incomplete exercise instance. Explicit terminal completion-with-omissions may preserve completed instances; the data contract must distinguish that from an unfinished session and store the omissions.
- **High RIR (WARN-4):** `6_plus` is observed but imprecise, not missing. Return `effort_too_easy_recalibration` with no automatic increase; unknown returns `effort_unknown_hold`. Neither earns the two-success advancement. Preserve the original value in history.
- **Calibration (WARN-5):** the 5% cap bounds automatic proposals, not user-entered actual work or a new independently accepted calibration. Manual selection must not masquerade as an engine recommendation. Every initial/recalibration target requires explicit confirmation of unit, load basis and actual equipment; bounds are enforced. A >20% upward change from the most recent comparable actual load additionally requires an outlier acknowledgement. Do not compare unrelated machines or same-pattern lifts as equivalent load. Grok's 60→70 kg example is 16.67%, so a >20% test alone cannot flag it; explicit calibration confirmation still applies. No calibration consumes progression evidence or certifies a load as safe.
- **Conditioning (WARN-6):** the initial two-bout template has an authored 30-minute per-bout ceiling and a 60-minute planned weekly ceiling. These are product-template limits, not physiological safety thresholds or WHO targets. Apply the PRD increment formula, then fit the proposed increase within both ceilings; no whole-minute increase fitting both means hold. Off-day conditioning is permitted and does not count as a strength session for nonconsecutive-day constraints. Extra-gym activity is separately reported context and does not complete a planned bout or enter its advancement window.
- **Starting conditioning dose (D4):** require the athlete to accept the planned bout duration. Offer 10 minutes initially, bounded downward by an explicitly reported shorter comfortable continuous bout. Allow a deliberate 1–20 minute starting choice. Do not infer a duration from posture score or silently equate recalled weekly activity with two completed bouts. The authored effort cue/band remains subject to the template review contract; the engine accepts the authored target rather than inventing it.
- **Time (D6):** stale-session cutoff is strictly more than 24 elapsed hours after start; a return review is triggered at 14 elapsed days or more since the latest comparable completed exposure. Use supplied UTC timestamps for elapsed arithmetic and athlete-local dates/timezone for scheduling. No inference from a future timestamp; reject inconsistent chronology. Starting a new comparator series invokes calibration, not reuse of stale prior success. The 20% outlier comparison is `(new - prior) / prior > 0.20` for positive prior comparable load; zero/unknown prior goes through explicit calibration, not division.
- **Equipment (D2):** barbell total includes the explicitly configured bar and collars plus symmetric plate pairs with available counts; a plate amount is per side. Dumbbells use an explicit sorted list of achievable per-hand loads; machine stacks use an explicit sorted list tied to the exact equipment ID. Never interpret a generic increment as both per-side and total. Deduplicate equivalent totals deterministically and choose the smallest achievable greater load satisfying the cap. Assistance has its own nonnegative supported range and no positive-load progression formula in V1.
- **Compiler choices (D3):** the user may select from compatible reviewed variants. Default ranking is authored preference order followed by stable exercise-version ID, independent of scans. Invalid/insufficient options return structured alternatives for acceptance. Schedule alternatives preserve as many selected days as possible, then rank by number of moved sessions and lexicographic weekday order; no automatic acceptance or missed-session doubling.

## Access and scope contracts still to freeze

- **BLOCK-1 accepted:** independent athlete identity must own a training subject without creating a fictitious practitioner. Coach links are optional, separately authorized relationships. Existing practitioner-owned clients remain unchanged. The data-owner proposal must define the optional legacy-client bridge, invitations/claims, RLS and revocation without converting clinician/coach authority implicitly.
- **BLOCK-2 partially accepted:** an explicit adult eligibility input is missing and will be required. The PRD excludes pregnancy-specific programming; that wording is not itself a finding that pregnancy makes general exercise unsafe. The product needs an explicit scope/support question and unavailable/specialized-review path for requests outside its supported population, with no diagnostic label. Exact clinical wording and clearance policy need qualified review; do not invent a postpartum cutoff or blanket contraindication.
- **WARN-1 accepted:** versioned eligibility branches and typed clearance constraints must exist before the live self-directed path. The compiler can be implemented against fixtures while this contract is prepared; synthetic eligibility is never a real-user clearance.
- **WARN-9 accepted:** optimistic revision conflicts between online athlete and coach writers are required for the first athlete release. Offline queue/replay is additional later work, not a reason to defer online conflict handling.

## Findings already covered or needing a narrower correction

- **WARN-7:** the PRD already separates capture quality, repeatability, validity and actionability. Make the adapter enforce that distinction. Existing `reliable` never establishes repeatability or a lift restriction. Optional preparation is chosen by preference/tolerance; it must not be extra volume generated from an unvalidated scan.
- **WARN-8:** normative precedence is already explicit, but add a prominent superseded-on-conflict header to every annex and remove conflicting candidate RIR/milestone defaults as they are reconciled. Test fixtures are owned by the normative contract.
- **NIT-1:** make the PR-02 fixture explicitly `3 × 6–8` performed as `8/8/8`; next accepted load target begins at `6/6/6` within the same range.
- **NIT-2:** conditioning prose must say “up to two minutes per bout”; the floor/cap formula controls.
- **NIT-3:** first matrix covers 8 weeks; all cycle lengths remain mandatory in Wave 3. No placeholder templates or reduced final acceptance.
- **NIT-4:** no e1RM implementation in the first slice; later descriptive metric requires an explicit equation/eligible domain. This does not remove it from the full plan.
- **NIT-5:** already covered: PRD explicitly calls 4 seconds per rep a planning heuristic, not a forced timer.
- **NIT-6:** freeze the first release to the two entry templates (strength/general fitness); reported experience affects familiarization questions, not unreviewed advanced loading. Intermediate low-rep and undulating templates retain their later contract/review gate.

## Proof requirements

Tests must cover each changed branch with concrete inputs and reason codes, including two ceiling successes versus ceiling then below-ceiling; first exposure at a new load; mixed loads; terminal omissions versus active/aborted session; recalled history; unknown versus `6_plus`; exact 20% versus greater-than-20%; conditioning 10→11, 20→22, 29→30 and 30→hold; off-day scheduling; and online conflicting writers. The lead must obtain an independent followup once these contracts and access decisions are frozen. No review status changes to “resolved” solely because this document exists.
